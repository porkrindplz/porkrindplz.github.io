Mike

Game developer in Ontario, Canada, with 9+ publicly released games made solo, in small teams, and on contract. I build prototypes fast and find the route to a core mechanic that holds up. Programming work centres on 3C, AI behaviours, interaction systems and inventory.

Prior to game dev, my career was spent designing processes and systems in healthcare and manufacturing environments, which is where the documentation habit, the rule-set thinking, and the skills for running collaborative sessions come from.
